1. TYPES OF HOME CLAIMS COVERED
- Fire & Lightning Damage: Covers structural repairs and replacement of belongings.
- Water Damage: Covers damage from sudden and accidental bursting of internal pipes.
- Burglary/Theft: Covers stolen belongings provided there are visible signs of forced entry.
- Personal Liability: Covers legal costs if a guest is injured on the insured property.

2. EXCLUSIONS (NOT COVERED)
- Natural disasters including earthquakes, floods, and tsunamis (requires a separate Catastrophe Add-on).
- Damage caused by pests, termites, or rodents.
- Gradual water leaks, mold, or lack of standard maintenance.
- Unexplained loss or misplacement of items.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR CLAIM PROCESSING
To process a Home Property Claim, the customer MUST provide:
- A detailed inventory list of damaged or stolen items.
- Original purchase receipts or proof of ownership for any single item valued over $1,000.
- A formal Police Report (MANDATORY for burglary/theft claims).
- A Fire Department incident report (MANDATORY for fire claims).
- Photographic or video evidence of the scene before cleanup occurs.

4. COVERAGE LIMITS
- High-Value Items: Jewelry, watches, and artwork are capped at a maximum payout of $2,500 per item unless scheduled separately.
- Alternative Accommodation: Up to $200 per day (maximum 14 days) if the home is deemed uninhabitable during repairs.
