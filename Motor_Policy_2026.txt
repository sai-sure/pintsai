1. TYPES OF MOTOR CLAIMS COVERED
- Own Damage (Collision): Covers damage to the insured vehicle due to an accident.
- Third-Party Liability: Covers damage to another person's vehicle or property, and bodily injury.
- Theft & Fire: Covers the loss of the vehicle due to theft, or damage caused by fire.
- Windscreen/Glass: Covers replacement of broken windows or windscreens (no deductible applies).

2. EXCLUSIONS (NOT COVERED)
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdowns, or flat tires.
- Accidents occurring while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Damage occurring while the vehicle was being used for commercial ride-sharing (e.g., Uber, Grab) unless a Commercial Rider is explicitly attached to the policy.
- Unreported accidents (all accidents must be reported to the insurer within 24 hours).

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR CLAIM PROCESSING
To process a Motor Claim, the customer MUST provide the following:
- A repair estimate from an authorized workshop.

4. DEDUCTIBLES AND LIMITS
- Standard Excess/Deductible: $500 per claim for Own Damage.
- Young/Inexperienced Driver Excess: An additional $1,500 applies if the driver is under 25 or has held their license for less than 2 years.
